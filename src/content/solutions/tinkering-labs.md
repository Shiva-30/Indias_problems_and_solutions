## How it works

1. Schools get funding under NITI Aayog's Atal Innovation Mission to set up a lab and run it.[^aim]
2. Students in Classes 6 to 12 work with robotics, 3D printing, sensors, AI and the Internet of Things, and take part in innovation challenges and hackathons.[^dina]

## Evidence so far

- Over 1.1 crore students take part in lab activities, and more than 16 lakh projects have been made, by official count.[^dina]
- An assessment of about 500 schools in 2023 found that 75% reported more positive student attitudes to science and technology, and over half ran lab sessions at least twice a week.[^aim]
- Not knowing how to set up a lab was the most common problem, reported by about 31% of schools. 70% had trained their teachers.[^aim]
