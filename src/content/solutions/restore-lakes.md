## How it works

1. Dredging removes silt and increases how much water a lake can store.[^iisc]
2. Encroachments are removed. Karnataka's 2014 lake conservation law aims to prevent and remove them and to restore lakes to recharge groundwater.[^iisc]
3. Constructed wetlands are built in lakes to help the water clean itself.[^iisc]

## Evidence so far

- At Jakkur Lake in Bengaluru, constructed wetlands and an algal pond helped remove nutrients from incoming water.[^iisc]
- Researchers say silt build-up and the loss of links between lakes have contributed to Bengaluru's frequent floods.[^iisc]
- Measured results on flood reduction and groundwater recharge from restored Bengaluru lakes are still limited.[^iisc]
