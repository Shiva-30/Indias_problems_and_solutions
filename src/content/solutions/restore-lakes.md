## How it works

1. Map old lakes, channels and floodplains
2. Remove encroachments and desilt
3. Protect the restored area in the city plan

## Evidence so far

- Restored lakes in Bengaluru now hold monsoon run-off
- Needs long-term upkeep and legal protection
