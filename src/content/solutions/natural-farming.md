## How it works

1. Farmer-to-farmer training in natural inputs
2. Diverse crops and year-round soil cover
3. Groups share tools and market produce together

## Evidence so far

- Andhra Pradesh runs one of the largest programmes of its kind
- Evidence on yields and incomes is still being gathered
