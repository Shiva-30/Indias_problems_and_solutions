## How it works

1. Farmers use natural inputs instead of chemical fertilisers and pesticides.[^gist]
2. Andhra Pradesh runs a community-managed natural farming programme.[^gist]

## Evidence so far

- A true cost accounting study of 12 villages in three regions of Andhra Pradesh, from 2020 to 2022, found natural farms had yields 11% higher on average and 49% higher net incomes, mainly because they spent less on inputs.[^gist]
- Natural farmers lost 33% fewer working days to illness, and villages with more natural farming had more women in work.[^gist]
- The study was led by GIST Impact with support from the Global Alliance for the Future of Food, and covered 12 villages. Larger independent studies would make the evidence stronger.[^gist]
