## How it works

1. Candidates file sworn affidavits
2. Affidavits are published online and in apps
3. Civil society analyses and spreads the information

## Evidence so far

- Disclosure has been mandatory since the early 2000s following Supreme Court rulings
- Voter awareness of this information is still limited
