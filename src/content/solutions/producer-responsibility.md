## How it works

1. Producers, importers and brand owners register on a central portal, along with plastic waste processors.[^cse]
2. They have targets for collection, recycling, recycled content and reuse of plastic packaging, under guidelines issued in February 2022.[^cse]
3. Recyclers issue certificates that companies use to show they met their targets.[^cse]

## Evidence so far

- CPCB audits of four recyclers in Gujarat, Maharashtra and Karnataka found about 6 lakh fake certificates.[^ngt]
- CSE found that importers make up 83% of registered companies but put only 9% of packaging on the market, and that no virgin plastic makers had registered.[^cse]
- Companies reported putting about 8 million tonnes of plastic packaging a year on the market, nearly double CPCB's estimate of 4.1 million tonnes for all plastic waste.[^cse]
