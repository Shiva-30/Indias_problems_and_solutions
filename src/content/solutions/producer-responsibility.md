## How it works

1. Producers register and report how much packaging they sell
2. They buy recycling certificates or run collection
3. Targets rise year by year

## Evidence so far

- Extended producer responsibility for plastic packaging became mandatory in 2022
- Watchdogs have raised concerns about fake certificates and weak checks
