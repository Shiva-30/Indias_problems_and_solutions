## How it works

1. Industrial trans fat in foods containing edible oils and fats is limited to 2% from January 1, 2022, down from 3% in 2021 and 5% before that.[^tfa]
2. Foods high in fat, sugar or salt cannot be sold to children in school canteens, hostels or within 50 metres of the school gate.[^school]
3. Makers of these foods cannot advertise to children on school premises or within 50 metres of the gate.[^school]

## Evidence so far

- Front-of-pack warning labels are still not in force. In September 2026 the Supreme Court questioned why the regulator had taken so long.[^sc]
