## How it works

1. Dogs are caught, sterilised and vaccinated under the Animal Birth Control Rules, 2023, which follow a capture, neuter, vaccinate and release approach.[^mongabay]
2. In August 2025 the Supreme Court ordered that dogs be dewormed and vaccinated as well, released back to the same areas, and fed at set spots in every ward.[^mongabay]

## Evidence so far

- The need is large: over 3.7 million dog bites were reported in 2024, and rabies is estimated to kill about 20,000 people a year in India.[^mongabay]
- Some cities report results, such as 83% of dogs sterilised and vaccinated in Lucknow, but most claims have not been independently checked.[^mongabay]
- A 2020 modelling study in Nature found that only high-intensity programmes cut dog numbers lastingly, and that dogs moving between areas weakens results. Experts say the 70% target has not been reached.[^mongabay]
