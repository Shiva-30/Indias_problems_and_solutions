## How it works

1. Trained facilitators from the nonprofit Breakthrough run a 45-minute session every three weeks during the school day.[^ipr]
2. The programme ran for about two and a half years, from 2014 to 2016, in Haryana government schools with the state government.[^ipr]

## Evidence so far

- A randomised trial covered over 14,000 students in 314 schools. The sessions turned about 16% of unequal attitudes into more equal ones.[^ipr]
- Boys behaved more equally too, for example helping more with housework, and the effects were as strong two years later.[^ipr]
- Punjab planned to roll the programme out in government schools in 2021, reaching about 6 lakh students a year.[^ipr]

[^ipr]: [India scales up program to combat gender inequality](https://www.ipr.northwestern.edu/news/2021/india-scales-up-program-to-combat-gender-inequality.html), Institute for Policy Research, Northwestern University, August 2021, on the study led by Seema Jayachandran.
