## How it works

1. Trained facilitators run sessions during school hours
2. Students discuss real situations from their own lives
3. Activities continue over two to three years

## Evidence so far

- A randomised evaluation in Haryana schools found students' gender attitudes became more progressive
- Effects on behaviour were also observed, though change in norms takes time
