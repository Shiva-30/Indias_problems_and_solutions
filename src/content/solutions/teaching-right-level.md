## How it works

1. Teachers first find out each child's actual reading and maths level, which many lack the tools to do in normal classes.[^ipa]
2. Children are grouped by level for part of the day and taught with activities suited to that level.[^ipa]
3. Pratham runs learning camps and also helps state governments bring the approach into regular schools.[^ipa]

## Evidence so far

- Several randomised evaluations in India, starting in 2001, by researchers including Abhijit Banerjee and Esther Duflo, found the approach consistently raises test scores.[^ipa]
- Pratham's two main models together reach about 5 crore students.[^ipa]
