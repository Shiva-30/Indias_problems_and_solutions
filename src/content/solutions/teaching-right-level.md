## How it works

1. A quick one-on-one test of reading and maths
2. Children grouped by level for part of the day
3. Regular re-testing and regrouping

## Evidence so far

- Randomised evaluations by J-PAL found clear learning gains
- Adopted by several Indian states and African countries
