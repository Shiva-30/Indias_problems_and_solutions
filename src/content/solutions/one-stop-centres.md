## How it works

1. A woman reaches a centre directly, or through the 181 Women Helpline, which runs around the clock.[^pib]
2. The centre arranges medical aid, police assistance, legal aid and counselling, and temporary shelter if needed.[^pib]
3. The helpline also connects women to hospitals and legal services and gives information on welfare schemes.[^pib]

## Evidence so far

- About 10,61,337 women had been helped through One Stop Centres since they started, up to the end of 2024.[^pib]
