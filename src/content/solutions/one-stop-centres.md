## How it works

1. Survivor reaches a centre directly or through the helpline
2. Staff arrange medical care and help file a complaint
3. Legal aid and counselling continue afterwards

## Evidence so far

- Centres operate in districts across India
- Reviews point to gaps in staffing and awareness
