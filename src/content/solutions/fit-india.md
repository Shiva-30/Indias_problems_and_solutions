## How it works

1. Schools are rated on daily physical education time, working playfields and staff fitness checks.[^gk]
2. Events such as the Fit India Freedom Run, Cyclothon and Plog Run invite everyone to walk, run or cycle.[^gk]
3. A mobile app offers fitness tests and health tracking.[^gk]

## Evidence so far

- The Prime Minister launched the movement on August 29, 2019, to fight the rising burden of diseases such as diabetes and heart disease. It is led by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports.[^gk]
- Activity levels are still falling: almost half of Indian adults were not active enough in 2022.[^activity]
