## How it works

1. Schools set daily activity time
2. Cities open parks, cycle lanes and open gyms
3. Workplaces encourage breaks and activity

## Evidence so far

- The Fit India Movement started in 2019
- Lasting change depends on safe spaces to walk, play and exercise
