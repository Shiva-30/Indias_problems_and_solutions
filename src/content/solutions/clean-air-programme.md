## How it works

1. Each of the 130 cities, in 24 states and union territories, writes an action plan.[^ians]
2. Grants are linked to performance. The cities had received ₹13,415.43 crore by December 2025.[^ians]

## Evidence so far

- 103 cities had lower PM10 levels in 2024-25 than in 2017-18. 64 cut them by more than 20%, and 25 by more than 40%.[^ians]
- Only 22 cities had PM10 below the national standard of 60 µg/m³.[^ians]
