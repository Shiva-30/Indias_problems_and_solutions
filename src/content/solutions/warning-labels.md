## How it works

1. Packs carry bold front-of-pack warnings when they are high in sugar, salt or saturated fat.[^chile]
2. Marketing of these products to children is restricted, and their sale in schools is banned.[^chile]

## Evidence so far

- Purchases of sugary drinks fell 23.7% after Chile's law, a study in PLOS Medicine found in 2020.[^chile]
- India's food regulator has proposed a red hexagonal warning label for foods high in sugar, fat and salt, with a year of voluntary compliance. The Supreme Court questioned the delay in September 2026.[^sc]
