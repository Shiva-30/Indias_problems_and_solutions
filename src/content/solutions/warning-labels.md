## How it works

1. Black warning symbols on the front of the pack
2. Bans on child-directed marketing for those products
3. Companies often reformulate to avoid the label

## Evidence so far

- Studies in Chile found people bought fewer sugary drinks after the law
- Several countries have since adopted similar labels
