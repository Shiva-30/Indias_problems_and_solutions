## How it works

1. States set up Exclusive Special Courts to try cases under the Act, and are asked to finish cases within two months as far as possible.[^act]
2. The state must protect victims and witnesses, give victims timely notice of hearings, and video record proceedings.[^act]
3. Victims and their dependents get immediate relief in cash or kind under a state scheme.[^act]

## Evidence so far

- The Act was amended in 2015, with changes in force from January 2016, and again in 2018.[^act]
- Enforcement is the weak link: at the end of 2019, 94% of cases of crimes against Scheduled Castes were pending in court, and the conviction rate was about 32%, against around 50% for all crimes under the Indian Penal Code.[^factly]
