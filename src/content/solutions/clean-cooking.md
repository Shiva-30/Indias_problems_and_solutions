## How it works

1. Poor households get an LPG connection under Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana, launched in May 2016.[^ians]
2. Refills are subsidised by ₹300 per 14.2 kg cylinder, for up to 9 refills a year in 2025-26.[^ians]

## Evidence so far

- About 10.6 crore families had joined by November 2025.[^ians]
- Use is rising but low: beneficiaries bought about 4.47 refills each in 2024-25, up from about 3 in 2019-20.[^ians]
- In a CEEW survey of rural households in six states, only 23% cooked with LPG alone. Using LPG only needs 7 to 8 refills a year, and 70% of households not using LPG said refill cost was a big problem.[^ceew]
