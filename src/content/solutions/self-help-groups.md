## How it works

1. Women from poor households form self-help groups that save and lend to members.[^i4i]
2. Group loans cost about 24% a year, far less than the 60% to 120% charged by moneylenders.[^i4i]
3. The National Rural Livelihoods Mission, running since 2012, uses the same model as Bihar's JEEViKA programme.[^i4i]

## Evidence so far

- By February 2025, 10.05 crore rural households had joined more than 90.90 lakh groups, and 1.48 crore women had declared earning at least ₹1 lakh a year by August 2025.[^factly]
- An evaluation of JEEViKA in 400 villages found member households had far less high-cost debt (₹5,000 against ₹14,500) and women were more likely to attend panchayat meetings. The author notes it was not a gold-standard trial.[^i4i]
