## How it works

1. Map every drain that flows into the river
2. Build and run treatment plants
3. Monitor water quality and penalise polluters

## Evidence so far

- Large investment has gone into treatment capacity on the Ganga
- Plants often run below capacity, so operation and maintenance matter as much as construction
