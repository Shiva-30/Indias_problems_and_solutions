## How it works

1. Sewage treatment plants are built in towns along the river.[^dte]
2. Pollution boards are meant to check that industries along the river comply with the rules.[^prs]

## Evidence so far

- A 2017 CAG audit found ₹2,134 crore lying unused with the National Mission for Clean Ganga, and delays in 26 projects worth ₹2,710 crore.[^prs]
- Only 63% of the required compliance checks on grossly polluting industries were done from 2011 to 2017.[^prs]
- In Uttarakhand, a later CAG audit found 12 of 44 plants releasing untreated wastewater into the Ganga, and only 3 plants meeting standards in late 2023, after ₹873.17 crore was spent.[^dte]
- Some plants are overloaded while others sit nearly idle: Devprayag's plant ran at 3% to 4% of capacity for lack of sewage reaching it.[^dte]
