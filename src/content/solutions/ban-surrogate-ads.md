## How it works

1. Regulators monitor advertisements on TV, online and at events
2. Complaints can be filed by anyone
3. Penalties for advertisers and endorsers

## Evidence so far

- India's 2022 advertising guidelines explicitly prohibit surrogate advertising
- Enforcement, especially at sports events and online, is still patchy
