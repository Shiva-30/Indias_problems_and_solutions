## How it works

1. The Central Consumer Protection Authority's 2022 guidelines prohibit indirect ads for products that cannot legally be advertised, including ads that use the same brand name, logo or colours.[^ccpa]
2. Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, the authority can fine makers, advertisers and endorsers up to ₹10 lakh, or ₹50 lakh for repeat violations.[^ccpa]
3. It can bar an endorser of a misleading ad from endorsing for up to one year, or three years for repeat violations.[^ccpa]

## Evidence so far

- A year after the ban, 41% of ads between overs in the 2023 Cricket World Cup were surrogate ads for smokeless tobacco, an ICMR-NICPR study found.[^icmr]
