## How it works

1. Private schools reserve one-fourth of their seats for children from economically weaker sections.[^i4i]
2. The government repays their fees, up to what it spends per child in public schools.[^i4i]

## Evidence so far

- When poorer children joined elite Delhi private schools, richer students became more generous, more egalitarian and less likely to discriminate against poorer peers. Direct personal contact seemed to drive the change.[^rao]
- Effects on richer students' marks were small: a slight dip in English and none in maths or Hindi.[^i4i]
- Private schools are more willing to take these children when fee repayments arrive on time and reliably.[^i4i]

[^rao]: Gautam Rao, [Familiarity does not breed contempt: generosity, discrimination, and diversity in Delhi schools](https://www.aeaweb.org/doi/10.1257/aer.20180044), American Economic Review 109(3), 2019.
