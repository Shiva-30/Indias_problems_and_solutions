## How it works

1. Private schools reserve a quarter of entry-level seats for disadvantaged children
2. Schools support new students academically and socially

## Evidence so far

- A study in Delhi found that richer students with poorer classmates became more generous and less discriminatory
- Implementation of the seat quota varies by state
