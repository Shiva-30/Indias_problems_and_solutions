## How it works

1. The scheme covers 78 districts in Gujarat, Maharashtra, Haryana, Karnataka, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh, with World Bank support of $450 million.[^wb]
2. It runs for six years from 2020 with a national outlay of ₹6,000 crore.[^dh]
3. Money goes into practices that save water, such as drip and sprinkler irrigation, mulching and switching crops.[^dh]

## Evidence so far

- In Karnataka, 1.82 lakh hectares were brought under water-saving practices, and water levels rose in 2023 in 255 of the 1,199 gram panchayats covered. The government said the scheme was one of several reasons.[^dh]
