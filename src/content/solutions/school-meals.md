## How it works

1. Government and government-aided schools serve a free cooked meal to children from pre-primary to Class 8.[^efa]
2. Meals must provide at least 450 calories and 12 g of protein in primary classes, and 700 calories and 20 g of protein in upper primary.[^efa]
3. The scheme aims to get children from poor families to attend school more regularly.[^i4i]

## Evidence so far

- Studies find school meals help keep girls in school longer and help fight hunger.[^i4i]
- Children whose mothers got free meals in primary school grew taller: their height deficits were 20% to 30% smaller, with larger gains in poor households.[^i4i]
- That study, published in Nature Communications in 2021, also found that girls who got the meals completed more years of school.[^i4i][^ifpri]
- Gaps remain: about 10.32 crore children were covered in 2024-25, children in private unaided schools and Classes 9 to 12 are left out, and a quarter of schools still cook with firewood.[^efa]
