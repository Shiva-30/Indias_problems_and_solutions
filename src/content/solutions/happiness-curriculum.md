## How it works

1. Classes use mindfulness, critical thinking, storytelling and play-based activities.[^brookings]
2. Teachers are not required to finish a syllabus. The aim is for children to take the ideas to heart.[^brookings]

## Evidence so far

- The curriculum runs in 1,030 Delhi government schools.[^brookings]
- Each class gets a 35-minute period every day.[^c360]
- A 2020 Brookings and Dream a Dream study, based on interviews and surveys, found better relationships in and out of class: students handled small disagreements more thoughtfully. It was not a randomised trial.[^c360]

[^brookings]: Helyn Kim, Vishal Talreja and Sreehari Ravindranath, [How do you measure happiness? Exploring the happiness curriculum in Delhi schools](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/how-do-you-measure-happiness-exploring-the-happiness-curriculum-in-delhi-schools/), Brookings, November 2019.
