## How it works

1. Mission Parivar Vikas began in 2016 in 146 high-fertility districts of Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand and Assam.[^pib]
2. It offers more contraceptive choices, such as the Antara injectable, and uses Saas Bahu Sammelans (meetings of mothers-in-law and daughters-in-law), awareness vans and kits for newly married couples.[^pib]

## Evidence so far

- Women with no schooling have 2.8 children on average, against 1.8 for women who completed Class 12.[^nfhs]
- Modern contraceptive use rose faster in Mission Parivar Vikas districts than elsewhere, so in 2021 the programme was extended to all districts of the seven states and six north-eastern states.[^pib]
- Unmet need for family planning is still 9.4%.[^nfhs]
