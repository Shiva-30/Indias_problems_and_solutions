## How it works

1. Girls supported to finish secondary school
2. Wider choice of contraceptives at health centres
3. Counselling for couples

## Evidence so far

- Educated women tend to marry later and have fewer children
- India's fertility rate has fallen to around replacement level nationally, but varies widely by state
