## How it works

1. Homes, shops and offices separate waste into six categories.[^tribune]
2. The city collects it door to door.[^tribune]
3. Wet and dry waste are processed and recycled separately.[^tribune]

## Evidence so far

- Indore reports 98% segregation at source, 100% door-to-door collection and 100% processing of its roughly 700 tonnes of wet and 1,200 tonnes of dry waste a day.[^tribune]
- It topped the national cleanliness survey, Swachh Survekshan, for the eighth year in a row in 2024-25.[^tribune]
- Nationally, more than 8,000 wards did not separate waste in 2020-21.[^fc]
