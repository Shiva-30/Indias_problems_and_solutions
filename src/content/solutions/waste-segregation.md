## How it works

1. Door-to-door collection in separate compartments
2. Wet waste composted or turned into biogas
3. Dry waste sorted and sold for recycling

## Evidence so far

- Indore has topped India's national cleanliness survey for several years running using this approach
- Success depends on steady public awareness and enforcement
