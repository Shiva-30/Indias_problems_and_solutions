## How it works

1. Farmer applies for a subsidy through the state portal
2. Drip lines installed with a filter and pump
3. Water and fertiliser go straight to the roots

## Evidence so far

- Studies report large water savings on crops like sugarcane and cotton
- Uptake is uneven and depends on subsidy access
