## How it works

1. Small and marginal farmers get 55% of the cost of micro-irrigation, and other farmers 45%, for up to 5 hectares each.[^pdmc]

## Evidence so far

- The Economic Survey 2020-21 linked micro-irrigation to 20% to 48% water savings, 11% to 19% fertiliser savings and 20% to 38% higher yields.[^pdmc]
- Coverage is still small: over 115 lakh hectares by July 2026, about 8.11% of India's net sown area.[^pdmc]
