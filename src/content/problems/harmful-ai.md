## Causes

- AI is the biggest driver of growth in data centre electricity use, which the IEA expects to more than double worldwide, from about 415 TWh in 2024 to around 945 TWh by 2030.[^iea]
- India's data centre capacity of 1.4 GW could grow about fivefold by 2030, and data centres could use 1.9% to 3.2% of the country's electricity, up from 0.8% in 2024.[^macq]
- India's AI governance guidelines of November 2025 do not propose a dedicated AI law. They start with voluntary commitments and amendments to existing laws.[^azb]

## Impact

- Data centre hubs such as Mumbai and Chennai already face severe water stress, and analysts say India needs a data centre policy that accounts for water.[^macq]
- In a McAfee survey, over 75% of Indian internet users had seen deepfake content in the past year, 25% had seen political deepfakes later found to be false, and at least 38% had come across a deepfake scam.[^mcafee]
