## Causes

- In 2020-21, more than 8,000 wards did not separate waste, and 2,636 of 90,617 wards had no door-to-door collection.[^fc]
- Official figures cover only registered facilities and miss the informal sector, so there is no reliable count of how much waste is collected.[^fc]

## Impact

- In 2020-21, only 47% of waste was treated. 27% went to landfill and 25.8% was unaccounted for.[^fc]
- Processing capacity has since risen from 16% of waste generated in 2014 to 80.17% in 2024, and 1,33,164 tonnes a day is now reported as processed.[^mohua]

[^fc]: [Does India really process 73% of its waste?](https://factchecker.in/context-check/does-india-really-process-73-of-its-waste-as-union-minister-claims-829905), FactChecker, August 2022, using the CPCB annual report on Solid Waste Management Rules for 2020-21.
