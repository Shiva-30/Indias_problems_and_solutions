## Causes

- Little separation of wet, dry and hazardous waste at home
- Not enough processing and recycling capacity
- Informal waste pickers working without support or safety

## Impact

- Landfill fires and toxic smoke
- Polluted groundwater and blocked drains
- Disease near dumpsites
