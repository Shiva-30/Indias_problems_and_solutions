## Causes

- Board exams ask students to learn large amounts of material and answer stock questions in set ways, and a student's worth is measured mostly by exam results.[^kar]
- The National Education Policy 2020 promises exams that do not need rote learning, but its added exams in Classes 3, 5 and 8 may keep children under pressure to perform.[^kar]

## Impact

- In 2023, among rural youth aged 14 to 18, only 43% could do a division sum, 57.3% could read simple English sentences, and about 45% could work out from a clock how long someone slept.[^aser]
- Student suicides rose from 6,654 to 13,044 over a decade, growing about 4% a year, double the rate for all suicides.[^ic3]
