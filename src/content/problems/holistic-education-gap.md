## Causes

- Exam-driven curriculum and coaching culture
- Large classes and overworked teachers
- Little training in teaching methods that build understanding

## Impact

- Students who memorise but cannot apply or question
- Stress, anxiety and student suicides
- Graduates without skills employers need
