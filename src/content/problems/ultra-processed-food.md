## Causes

- Retail sales of ultra-processed food grew 13.37% a year from 2011 to 2021. Drinks, chocolate and sweets, and ready-to-eat foods sold the most by volume.[^who]
- The Economic Survey says misleading ads and celebrity endorsements shape what people buy, and calls for stricter limits on marketing unhealthy food to children under 18.[^es]
- India still has no mandatory front-of-pack warning label. In September 2026 the Supreme Court asked the food regulator why it had not acted since starting work in 2022, and questioned its plan for a year of voluntary compliance.[^sc]

## Impact

- The Economic Survey links the shift to ultra-processed diets with obesity, chronic inflammatory disorders, heart disease and mental disorders.[^es]
- WHO has recommended that India define foods high in fat, sugar and salt and tax them more under GST.[^who]
