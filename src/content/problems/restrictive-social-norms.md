## Causes

- Most marriages are arranged by families. In a CSDS-KAS survey of young people aged 15 to 34, 84% said they had an arranged marriage.[^csds]
- More than one in three young people disapproved of inter-caste marriage.[^csds]
- Many families expect women to cover their faces or stay out of sight of men (purdah or ghoonghat). This cuts across religions: burqas and headscarves among Muslims, and saris or dupattas over the face among Hindus and others.[^veil]
- 64% of Indians said it is very important to stop women in their community marrying into another caste.[^pew]

## Impact

- Girls lose school: in north Karnataka, rules on girls' dress and movement after puberty, and fear over their 'purity', made some girls drop out.[^school]
- 23% of women aged 20 to 24 were married before 18 in 2019-21, down from 27% five years earlier.[^nfhs]
- There is progress: 92% of married women now take part in household decisions, up from about 74% in 2015-16.[^nfhs]
