## Causes

- A surrogate ad gets around a ban by appearing to advertise something legal, using the brand name, logo, colours or layout of the banned product.[^ccpa]
- The Central Consumer Protection Authority prohibited surrogate ads in June 2022. Fines under the Consumer Protection Act go up to ₹10 lakh, or ₹50 lakh for repeat violations, and endorsers can be barred for up to three years.[^ccpa]
- Even so, surrogate tobacco ads filled 41% of ad slots between overs in the 2023 World Cup, a year after the ban.[^icmr]

## Impact

- Researchers warned of the influence of these ads on adolescents and young people.[^icmr]
- In the 2019 Global Youth Tobacco Survey, 18% of students aged 13 to 15 noticed tobacco ads or promotions at shops, and nearly one in five had used tobacco at some point.[^gyts]
