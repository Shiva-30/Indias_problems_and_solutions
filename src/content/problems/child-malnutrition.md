## Causes

- Only about 11% of children aged 6 to 23 months get a minimum acceptable diet, barely better than the 9.6% in NFHS-4.[^iasbaba]
- 21.4% of babies were born with low birth weight in 2017.[^gbd]
- Poor nutrition in pregnancy and early childhood leads to lasting health, cognitive and economic disadvantages.[^unicef]

## Impact

- Malnutrition was linked to 68.2% of deaths of children under five in India in 2017, the leading risk factor in every state.[^gbd]
- It was the leading risk factor for lost health across all ages, responsible for 17.3% of India's total disease burden.[^gbd]
