## Causes

- Free or flat-rate electricity for farm pumps
- Water-hungry crops such as paddy in dry regions
- No limits on how much a landowner can draw

## Impact

- Wells dry up and farmers pay for deeper borewells
- Drinking water shortages in summer
- Arsenic and fluoride contamination in some aquifers
