## Causes

- Irrigation takes 215 of the 247 billion cubic metres pumped each year.[^cgwb]
- Punjab gives farmers free electricity for pumps. One study estimates that charging the real cost of power would cut water drawn by electric pumps there by 59%.[^gupta]
- Subsidies and assured government buying of rice have pushed up the area under paddy, a water-hungry crop.[^stubble]

## Impact

- 751 assessment units (11.13%) were over-exploited in 2024, drawing more water than is recharged.[^factly]
- Groundwater supplies over 80% of India's drinking and household water, so falling water tables hit homes as well as farms.[^wb]
- Arsenic has been found in groundwater in parts of 230 districts in 25 states, and fluoride in 469 districts in 27 states, above safe limits in some pockets.[^arsenic]
