## Causes

- Political parties picking candidates for money and muscle power
- Slow courts, so cases stay pending for years
- Low voter awareness of candidate affidavits

## Impact

- Laws and budgets made without expert understanding
- Lower public trust in politics
- Corruption and weak accountability
