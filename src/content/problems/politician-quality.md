## Causes

- Candidates facing criminal cases win more often. In 2024, they had a 15.3% chance of winning, against 4.4% for candidates with clean records.[^adr]
- Cases move slowly. In November 2022, 5,097 criminal cases were pending against MPs and MLAs, and 2,122 of them had been pending for more than five years.[^amicus]

## Impact

- 251 of 543 MPs elected in 2024 (46%) declared criminal cases, up from 233 (43%) in 2019 and 162 (30%) in 2014.[^adr]
- 170 MPs (31%) declared serious cases, such as murder, attempted murder, rape or kidnapping.[^adr]
- Formal education is less of a gap than often assumed: 77% of the 2024 winners are graduates or above, and 19% studied up to Class 5 to 12.[^edu]
