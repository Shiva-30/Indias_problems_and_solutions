## Causes

- Planning that prioritises vehicles over pedestrians and cyclists
- Concrete over green cover, lakes and open ground
- Little thought for heat, accessibility or wildlife corridors

## Impact

- Less physical activity and more road accidents
- Hotter cities and more flooding
- Isolation and stress from crowded, unsafe public spaces
- Lost habitat for birds and animals
