## Causes

- In six big cities studied from 2001 to 2024, the more an area was built up, the more heat stress rose.[^cse]
- In Chennai, green cover fell by close to 14% over two decades while concrete cover doubled.[^cse]

## Impact

- 4,61,312 road crashes in 2022 killed 1,68,491 people, 11% more than in 2021.[^morth]
- Pedestrian deaths rose 12.7% to 32,825, and 74,897 people on two-wheelers died.[^morth]
- Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Chennai and Bengaluru are not cooling down at night as much as they did in 2001 to 2010.[^cse]

[^morth]: [Analysing the MoRTH road accidents report for 2022](https://opencity.in/analysing-the-morth-road-accidents-report-for-2022/), OpenCity, using the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways report Road Accidents in India 2022.
[^cse]: [Indian cities no longer cooling down at night, finds CSE study](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/amp/story/urbanisation/indian-cities-no-longer-cooling-down-at-night-finds-cse-study), Down To Earth, May 2024.
