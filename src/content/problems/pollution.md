## Causes

- Indian cities generate 72,368 million litres of sewage a day, but plants treat only about 20,235 million litres, around 28%.[^stp]
- Installed treatment capacity covers only 43.9% of the sewage generated, and not all of it runs.[^stp]

## Impact

- Pollution was linked to 2.4 million deaths in India in 2019: 1.6 million from air pollution, 0.5 million from water pollution, 0.23 million from lead and 0.16 million from workplace pollution.[^lancet]
- 311 stretches on 279 rivers in 30 states and union territories were polluted in 2022, measured by organic pollution, down from 351 stretches in 2018.[^rivers]
