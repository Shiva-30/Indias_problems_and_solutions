## Causes

- Untreated sewage and industrial effluent released into rivers
- Heavy use of pesticides and chemical fertilisers
- Vehicle, factory and construction emissions
- Weak monitoring and enforcement of pollution rules

## Impact

- Water-borne and respiratory disease
- Poisoned soil and lower crop quality
- Loss of fish, birds and other wildlife
