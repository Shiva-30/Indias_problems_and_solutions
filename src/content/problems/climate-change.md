## Causes

- Greenhouse gas emissions from coal power, transport and industry, in India and worldwide
- Deforestation and loss of wetlands
- Cities and farms not yet adapted to new weather patterns

## Impact

- Heatwaves that harm outdoor workers, children and the elderly
- Crop losses and food price shocks
- Floods, cyclones and coastal erosion displacing families
