## Causes

- Climate change is driven by greenhouse gases from every country. Within India, energy (power, transport and industry fuel) was the largest source in 2020, at 2,238 million tonnes CO2-equivalent.[^bur]
- Agriculture added 406 million tonnes and industrial processes 239 million tonnes.[^bur]
- Forests and other land soaked up about 22% of India's CO2 emissions in 2020, so losing them adds to the problem.[^bur]

## Impact

- India ranked 6th most affected country in the world by extreme weather from 1993 to 2022, with about 80,000 deaths and $180 billion in losses.[^cri]
- Heat cost India 181 billion potential working hours in 2023, half as many again as in the 1990s, and an estimated $141 billion in lost income, about half of it in farming.[^lancet]
- From 2014 to 2023, 42.7% of India's land had at least one month of extreme drought a year, nearly double the 1950s.[^lancet]
